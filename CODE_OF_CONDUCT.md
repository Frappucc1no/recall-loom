# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

RecallLoom aims to be a practical, respectful project for people building with
AI tools.

RecallLoom 希望成为一个务实、尊重他人、适合 AI 工具用户参与的开源项目。

## Expected Behavior / 期望行为

- Be clear, factual, and respectful.
- 表达清楚、基于事实、尊重他人。
- Assume good intent, but make technical concerns explicit.
- 默认善意，但要把技术问题说清楚。
- Keep discussions focused on the project, the user workflow, and the code.
- 讨论聚焦项目、用户工作流和代码。
- Share reproducible details when reporting problems.
- 报告问题时提供可复现信息。
- Redact sensitive paths, secrets, customer data, and personal project context.
- 对敏感路径、密钥、客户数据和个人项目上下文做脱敏。

## Unacceptable Behavior / 不接受的行为

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- 骚扰、侮辱、威胁或人身攻击。
- Publishing personal or sensitive information without permission.
- 未经允许发布个人或敏感信息。
- Repeated off-topic comments after a maintainer asks to stop.
- 在维护者提醒后仍持续发布偏题内容。
- Spam, deceptive links, or malicious examples.
- 垃圾信息、欺骗性链接或恶意示例。
- Security reports posted publicly before a maintainer has a chance to respond.
- 在维护者有机会响应前公开发布安全漏洞细节。

## Reporting / 报告

For conduct concerns, open a brief issue asking for maintainer contact without
sharing personal details publicly. For security concerns, follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.

行为问题请开一个简短 issue 请求维护者联系方式，不要公开个人细节。安全问题请遵循 [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)，不要直接开公开 issue。

## Enforcement / 执行

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, block users, or take
other moderation steps when needed to keep the project usable and safe.

为保持项目可用和安全，维护者可在必要时编辑或删除评论、关闭 issue、屏蔽用户，或采取其他管理措施。
